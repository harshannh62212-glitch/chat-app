Two workstreams

Project 2 — AEGIS-V1. External cargo transport from a landing area to a lunar base. This website is that mission dashboard: CAD inspection, an 850 m route animation, and a 1/6 g payload calculator.

Project 1 — cargo module. A 40 ft by 3 m cylindrical logistics module, a 14-day LIFO packing list, and a gantry-unloading simulation. The current manifest has 22 items and 748.5 kg. Center of mass is about −0.912 m along the length, −0.022 m lateral, and +0.002 m vertical.

What “prototype” means

  • The WebGL autopilot simulator plans with a LiDAR occupancy map, A*, and pure pursuit, plus a proximity supervisor that can reverse.
  • An optional local language model can bias the planner with a dodge of none, left, or right. It must not dodge into a pit or a steep slope to avoid a rock.
  • An imitation network can run in the browser from rover_pilot.onnx. No flight recording has been trained into that file yet.
  • The React rover dashboard shows simulated telemetry until a Pi link exists.
  • Classroom hardware trials and the NASA HUNCH evaluation are still ahead (targets Q1 2027 and spring 2027).

Numbers that are easy to mix up

6.39 kg
Classroom demonstrator curb mass. Use this for the rover you can inspect in CAD and the sim.
500 kg
Full-scale cargo scenario in the homepage calculator. Lunar weight at 1.622 m/s² would be about 811 N. That is not the built rover.
748.5 kg
Project 1 module manifest, 22 items, packed LIFO so day-1 cargo is near the hatch.
850 m
Scripted homepage route from the touchdown pad to the base gateway. It is an animation, not a closed-loop drive.

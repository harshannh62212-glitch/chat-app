Left panel — Problem, requirements & sizing

1. Mission problem

Lunar surface operations require autonomous, hazard-tolerant transport of cargo and science payloads between the 40-ft logistics module and EVA work sites—eliminating astronaut manual haul overhead.

2. Design constraints & environmental drivers

Parameter Design value Engineering impact
Gravity (Moon vs. Earth) 1.622 m/s² (⅙ g) vs. 9.81 m/s² Sized for Earth (78.5 N) to guarantee 6× margin on Moon (13 N)
Gross vehicle mass 8.0 kg (2.85 kg dry + 5.0 kg payload) Structural truss & actuator load baseline
Max slope & traction 25° grade · μ ≈ 0.65 (regolith simulant) Dictates track contact patch and stall torque

Center panel — Architecture, trade study & subsystems

1. Selected architecture: 6WD tracked skid-steer

Chassis: Modular PETG space-frame truss (4 perimeters, 35–40% gyroid infill) with brass heat-set inserts (M3/M4 installed at 230°C).

Trade study weights: Slope 25%, Sensors 20%, Torque 15%, Dust/Power 15%, Fab 15%, Fit 10%

  • 6WD tracked skid-steer (score: 94/100 — selected): Best flotation on loose regolith, stable sensor horizon, compact cargo envelope.
  • 4WD wheeled skid-steer (score: 65/100): Low complexity, but high sinkage/slip on loose simulant.
  • Rocker-bogie (score: 65/100): Superior obstacle climb, but exceeds mass budget and classroom fabrication schedule.

2. Drivetrain & torque sizing

Earth demo @ 25°, sprocket radius 45 mm

  • Required pull force: ≈ 80 N total (18.4 kg·cm stall torque per motor; ≥ 29.4 kg·cm at 1.6× safety factor).
  • Selected actuator: Dual JGB37-550 12V planetary motors (30:1 / 50:1), 35 kg·cm stall, 160–200 RPM (0.7–0.9 m/s), 6 mm D-shaft with quadrature encoders.

3. Hardware evolution (dropped vs. kept)

Subsystem Dropped baseline Upgraded selection
Motor driver L298N (current saturation / thermal drop) Cytron MDD10A (10 A cont. / 30 A peak)
5V power rail LM2596 3A buck (Pi brownout on LiDAR spin-up) Dual isolated XL4015 (5A cont. / 8A peak @ 5.1V) / Pololu D24V50F5
Gearbox & structure 1:10 nylon spur (stripped teeth) & brittle PLA JGB37-550 metal planetary + PETG printed tracks/sprockets
Compute split Single-board bottleneck Arduino UNO R4 WiFi (hard real-time PWM) + Raspberry Pi (vision/nav)

4. Power & autonomy stack

  • Power bus: 12.8V 4S LiFePO₄ (3–6 Ah, 1.5–2.5 hr runtime). 5V budget draws 3.5 A nominal / 4.75 A peak (Pi 3.5 A + YDLIDAR 1.0 A + cam 0.25 A), protected by motor-bus TVS diodes and opto-isolated PWM.
  • Navigation: YDLIDAR occupancy grid + A* global planner + Pure Pursuit controller, backed by an optional ONNX neural pilot, JSON dodge assist (none | left | right), and proximity auto-reverse supervisor.
  • Mission control: this site, aegisv1.vercel.app.

Right panel — Verification, test campaign & closed NCRs

1. Iterative engineering progression

  1. Phase 1 (Mockup): Cardboard prototype to lock wheelbase, CG, and track vs. wheel geometry.
  2. Phase 2 (CAD & fab): Fusion 360 truss, sprockets, and sensor mast printed in PETG.
  3. Phase 3 (Digital twin): Physics sim verifying 25° slope climb, track slip, and crater/pit guard.
  4. Phase 4 (Bench & FMEA): High-load PWM stress testing and non-conformance report (NCR) closure.
  5. Phase 5 (CDR → FDR): Full hardware-in-the-loop surface cargo run.

2. Simulation & hardware bench verification

  • Digital twin (run_nav_scenarios.py): Zero-collision pit avoidance (exit 0), CARGO RUN autonomous pallet docking, and automated CSV telemetry extraction (extract_sim_metrics.py).
  • Physical bench: Unloaded PWM sweep with zero driver faults and clean sensor streams.
  • 25° loaded incline crawl drawing < 12 A aggregate current.
  • 150 consecutive hard-stall cycles with zero MCU brownouts.

3. Closed non-conformance reports (FMEA / NCRs)

NCR Root cause Corrective action Result
NCR-001 MCU reset on hard stall (back-EMF) Dual isolated 5V rails, TVS diode clamping, optical PWM isolation PASS: 150 stalls, logic ripple < 22 mV
NCR-002 Stripped spur gear on 22° loose fill Upgraded to 1:31.6 all-metal planetary + software current foldback PASS: 28° crawl for 4 hrs; zero tooth wear
NCR-003 Driver thermal trip at 42 min enclosed Chimney vents, 304 mesh dust screen, Al heat spreader PASS: 3 hrs @ 45°C ambient; MOSFET ≤ 64°C
NCR-004 LiDAR false stops in direct solar glare 3D-printed glare cowls, amplitude filtering, camera cross-check PASS: 100/100 runs under halogen sun simulator

Autonomy & logistics

  • Classical driver: YDLIDAR occupancy grid, A* with rock inflation and pit guards, pure pursuit, in viewer.html.
  • Supervisor: proximity auto-reverse before contact.
  • Optional assist: local Ollama model hunch-rover-nav returns only {"dodge":"none"|"left"|"right"} and must not dodge into a pit or steep slope. ONNX imitation pilot rover_pilot.onnx loads in the browser when a trained file is present.
  • Project 1 cargo module: 40 ft × 3 m cylinder, 14-day LIFO manifest — 22 items, 748.5 kg. Center of mass [−0.912, −0.022, +0.002] m. Earth weight 7340.3 N, lunar weight 1214.1 N at 1.622 m/s².
  • WebGL vehicle catalog: rocker-bogie demonstrator curb mass 6.39 kg (6× Greartisan 12 V 100 RPM, GOLDENMATE 12 V 10 Ah / 128 Wh, Raspberry Pi 5, Arduino Uno REV3, YRDZXG 5 A buck, Cytron MDD10A, YDLIDAR X2L, dual Arducam OV5647, cargo arm). HQST 100 W panel stays on the hangar dock and is not in that curb mass. The 8.0 kg figure above is the tracked hardware sizing baseline.

Architecture & subsystems

6WD tracked skid-steer (94/100 — selected) over 4WD wheeled (65) and rocker-bogie (65): flotation on loose regolith, stable sensor horizon, compact cargo envelope. PETG space-frame truss, 35–40% gyroid, brass inserts at 230°C.

Torque (Earth @ 25°, 45 mm sprocket): ≈80 N pull; JGB37-550 12 V planetary (30:1 / 50:1), 35 kg·cm stall, 160–200 RPM. Upgrades: Cytron MDD10A, dual isolated XL4015 5 V rails, metal planetary + PETG tracks, Arduino UNO R4 WiFi + Raspberry Pi split.

Power & nav: 12.8 V LiFePO₄ 3–6 Ah; 5 V at 3.5 A nominal / 4.75 A peak. YDLIDAR grid + A* + Pure Pursuit; optional ONNX pilot; JSON dodge (none|left|right); proximity auto-reverse.

Digital twin & simulation

What the sim runs

  • Classical stack: YDLIDAR X2L → log-odds occupancy → A* replan → pure pursuit; rock inflation and pit guards in viewer.html.
  • Safety: Proximity supervisor with auto-reverse before impact.
  • Optional layers: Ollama hunch-rover-nav dodge bias; ONNX rover_pilot.onnx imitation pilot from recorded demos.
  • Logistics (Project 1): 40-ft cylindrical module, 14-day LIFO manifest — 22 items, 748.5 kg total, CoM as in brief table.
  • Rocker-bogie demonstrator in sim: catalog curb 6.39 kg (Greartisan 6×100 RPM, GOLDENMATE 128 Wh, Pi 5, MDD10A, YDLIDAR mast, cargo arm); HQST 100 W panel on hangar dock, not on rover mass.
